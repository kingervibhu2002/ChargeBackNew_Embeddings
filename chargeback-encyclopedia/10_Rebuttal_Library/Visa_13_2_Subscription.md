# Rebuttal Template: Visa Reason Code 13.2 — Cancelled Recurring Transaction

## About This Reason Code

Visa Reason Code 13.2 is filed when a cardholder claims they cancelled a recurring subscription or service, but the merchant continued to charge them. This is one of the most contested chargeback types because the cardholder's memory of cancellation often conflicts with the merchant's CRM records. Your defense must prove two things: (1) your cancellation policy was clearly disclosed at the time of sign-up, and (2) no valid cancellation request was received through any channel prior to the disputed charge.

A strong 13.2 defense is built on documentation of the cancellation policy presented at signup and a CRM record showing no cancellation event before the billing date.

---

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] Screenshot of cancellation policy displayed at checkout or sign-up
- [ ] Terms of Service or subscription agreement showing cancellation terms
- [ ] CRM or cancellation log showing no cancellation request received before the charge date
- [ ] Reminder emails sent before the billing date (if applicable)
- [ ] Confirmation email sent when subscription began (showing billing terms)
- [ ] Payment history showing prior accepted charges
- [ ] Customer service communication log (if cardholder contacted you)
- [ ] Authorization record for the disputed charge

## Merchant Notes

- If you have an easy, self-service cancellation method (account portal button, one-click cancel), document it and show that the cardholder did not use it. Issuers view self-service cancellation options favorably — they indicate merchant transparency.
- If the cardholder did submit a cancellation request but it was after the charge date, acknowledge this in your letter and note the timeline explicitly. Many 13.2 disputes arise from customers cancelling after the billing date but claiming they cancelled before it.
- If you refunded after the cancellation was received (even if it was late), note that the refund was issued and provide the credit reference number. This may not win the dispute but demonstrates good faith.
- Visa requires that recurring billing be clearly disclosed with the billing amount, frequency, and cancellation terms at the time of consent. Ensure your signup flow meets this standard to strengthen all future 13.2 responses.
