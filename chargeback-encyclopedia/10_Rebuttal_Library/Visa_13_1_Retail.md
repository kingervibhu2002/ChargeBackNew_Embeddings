# Rebuttal Template: Visa Reason Code 13.1 — Merchandise / Services Not Received (Physical Goods)

## About This Reason Code

Visa Reason Code 13.1 is filed when a cardholder claims they did not receive the merchandise or services they paid for. For physical goods, this is almost always a delivery dispute. The cardholder may have genuinely not received the package, or this may be a "porch piracy" situation misrepresented as a merchant failure, or in some cases friendly fraud. Your defense must provide verifiable, carrier-certified proof that the item was delivered to the address the cardholder confirmed at checkout.

---

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] Carrier tracking number showing delivery status
- [ ] Carrier's official delivery confirmation (printed from carrier website)
- [ ] Delivery timestamp and address
- [ ] Proof of cardholder's confirmed shipping address (checkout record, order confirmation)
- [ ] Signed proof of delivery (if signature was required or obtained)
- [ ] Shipping label or packing slip showing the address used
- [ ] Authorization record for the transaction
- [ ] Order confirmation email sent to cardholder

## Merchant Notes

- Always print carrier tracking from the official carrier website (ups.com, fedex.com, usps.com). A screenshot from your own shipping software alone may not be accepted. The carrier's website record is independently verifiable.
- For high-value orders ($200+), require signature confirmation as a standard policy. This eliminates "not received" disputes almost entirely.
- If the cardholder's billing address and shipping address differ, explain why (e.g., they entered a work address). Lack of address match is not automatically disqualifying, but you should address it proactively.
- If the carrier shows "delivered" but the cardholder insists otherwise, note in your letter that the cardholder should contact their local carrier office or file a carrier claim — this shifts the burden appropriately and demonstrates good faith.
- Never attempt to deliver to an address different from what the cardholder entered, as this weakens the 13.1 defense significantly.
