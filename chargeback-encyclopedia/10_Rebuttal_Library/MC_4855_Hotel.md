# Rebuttal Template: Mastercard Reason Code 4855 — Goods or Services Not Provided (Hotel)

## About This Reason Code

Mastercard Reason Code 4855 is used in the hotel and hospitality context when a cardholder disputes a hotel charge, often claiming they did not check in, did not authorize a no-show fee, did not agree to a deposit charge, or assert the room was not as described. This code applies to lodging charges that the cardholder refuses to accept after the fact.

Hotel chargebacks are uniquely defended through check-in records, signed registration cards, room folios, and loyalty program data — all of which establish that the guest physically arrived, was assigned a room, and used hotel services.

---

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] Signed guest registration card / check-in form
- [ ] Property Management System (PMS) check-in and check-out record
- [ ] Room folio / itemized bill showing charges
- [ ] Room assignment record
- [ ] Key card issuance record (if available)
- [ ] Authorization record (pre-authorization and final charge)
- [ ] No-show or cancellation policy presented at booking
- [ ] Reservation confirmation email sent to cardholder
- [ ] Loyalty program activity (points earned, redemption) if applicable

## Merchant Notes

- Signed registration cards are gold-standard evidence for hotel disputes. Scan and archive them immediately after checkout and retain for at least 18 months.
- For OTA bookings (Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com), obtain the guest's name, email, and card last-four from the OTA and confirm they match the cardholder in the dispute. OTA bookings can complicate identity verification.
- If a third party booked on behalf of the guest (corporate travel, travel agent), include the third-party authorization form showing the cardholder authorized the third party to book using their card.
- Key card access logs (showing door access events in the room) are supplemental evidence if available from your property management system.
