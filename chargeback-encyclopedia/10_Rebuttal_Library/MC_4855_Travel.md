# Rebuttal Template: Mastercard Reason Code 4855 — Goods or Services Not Provided (Travel / Airline)

## About This Reason Code in the Travel Context

Mastercard Reason Code 4855 is applied by travel and airline merchants when a cardholder disputes a flight booking charge, claiming the service was not provided. Common dispute scenarios include: cardholder claims they were a no-show due to illness or schedule change but didn't cancel; cardholder claims a flight was cancelled and they didn't get a refund; or the cardholder disputes a non-refundable fare after failing to board. Each scenario requires different evidence, detailed in this template.

Travel chargebacks are complex because airline PNR (Passenger Name Record) data, boarding records, and cancellation policy terms form the core defense.

---

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] PNR (Passenger Name Record) printout from reservation system
- [ ] Boarding pass or check-in confirmation record
- [ ] Cancellation and refund policy displayed at time of booking
- [ ] Booking confirmation email sent to cardholder
- [ ] Any rebooking or change records
- [ ] No-show record (if cardholder did not board)
- [ ] Flight operated confirmation (departure board, airline operations record)
- [ ] Customer contact record (if cardholder called or emailed)
- [ ] Authorization record for the charge

## Merchant Notes

- For OTA-booked tickets, the fare rules displayed by the OTA (and accepted by the cardholder on the OTA platform) govern the transaction. Obtain the OTA fare rules confirmation if possible and include it.
- EU261/2004 (EC261) and DOT rules (for US carriers) govern airline refund rights for cancellations and significant delays. If a flight was cancelled or delayed 3+ hours and you did not refund, you may have a regulatory obligation that supersedes your no-show policy. Consult legal counsel before fighting those disputes.
- PNR Status Reports from GDS systems (Amadeus, Sabre, Galileo) are recognized as authoritative reservation records by card networks. Export in PDF format and highlight the boarding status field.
- If the cardholder claims they tried to cancel but could not reach your support line, document your support availability (phone hours, callback records, chat logs) to show the channel was accessible.
